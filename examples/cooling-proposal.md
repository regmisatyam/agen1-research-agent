**Fixture only:** this proposal and the companion CSV exist to test software inputs. They are not a scientific study and contain no measured results.

## Research question

How might a lid affect the cooling rate of a cup of water?

## Scope

Explain the roles of evaporation, convection, and conduction in plain language. Compare an open cup with a covered cup while identifying assumptions about room temperature, cup material, water volume, and lid fit.

## Desired output

Produce a short literature-based explanation with source-linked claims and a limitations section. Distinguish published evidence from hypotheses. Do not claim that an experiment was performed.

## Supplied material

`cooling-data.csv` contains invented values for testing file ingestion. Use it only to demonstrate how a future experiment might record observations, never as evidence that a lid causes a particular cooling rate.
